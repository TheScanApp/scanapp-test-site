The ScanApp Receiving Solution

Know what was shipped, validate what physically arrived and resolve discrepancies before inventory is updated.

SHIPPED

Power Shipped shows what the OEM says is on the way and matches shipment information to dealership purchase orders before receiving begins.

SCAN

Scan parts as they arrive. ScanApp compares the physical receipt against purchase-order and OEM shipment data in real time.

REVIEW

Review receiving results, identify discrepancies and adjust quantities when necessary before the receipt is approved.

VERIFY

Select completed receipts and verify the final quantities. Verified Scans creates a controlled checkpoint before the DMS is updated.

RECEIPT

Power Receipt provides the daily exception view. Quorum purchase orders update automatically; PBS and Reynolds customers currently complete the final PO receipt manually.

Manage receiving by exception

  • Power Shipped matches OEM shipment data to dealership purchase orders and shows expected inbound inventory.
  • Part numbers, shipped quantities and DMS quantities are compared before receiving begins.
  • Supersessions, part issues and quantity issues are surfaced for review.
  • Physical receipt activity is captured in real time as parts are scanned.
  • Users can adjust quantities when the physical receipt does not match the expected receipt.
  • Receipt statuses include Received as Ordered, Short Shipped, Over Shipped, Shipped Without Order and Manually Assigned.
  • Completed receipts are verified before they move to the final receipt stage.
  • Power Receipt shows the status of daily receipts and highlights the exceptions that require attention.

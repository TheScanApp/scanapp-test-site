ScanApp is NOT a scanner.

ScanApp is a complete OEM parts importation and control tool.

Scanning is only one small part of the process. ScanApp brings together DMS purchase orders, OEM shipment data, physical receiving activity and OEM invoice information, then matches, validates and tracks the transaction through the workflow.

The scanner captures the part. ScanApp controls the information.

2. Email Setup — Office 365

Naming rule: Always use the full dealer number, including the leading zero, in dealer email addresses.

Create Contact

  • Create contact: DealerNumber@thescan.app

Create Distribution Group

  1. Group name: DealerNumber DealerName.
  2. Add Dave as Owner.
  3. Add Dave as a Member.
  4. Set group email to DealerNumber@powersystemsinc.ca.
  5. Check “Allow people outside of my organization to send email to this Distribution group.”
  6. Save/create the group.
  7. After the group has been created, add Sean as a Member.
  8. Add DealerNumber@thescan.app as a Member.
  9. Save and verify the complete setup.
Important: Add Sean and the @thescan.app contact after the group is initially created. Adding them during group creation has produced an error. The exact cause is unknown; it may relate to using a newly created contact or the group naming convention.

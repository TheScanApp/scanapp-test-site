ScanApp LPO Management

Track GM LPO parts from shipment by GM through receipt by the Parts Department and optional issuance to a service RO.

LPO Management

  • GM shipment data automatically provides incoming LPO information.
  • Receive LPO parts using ScanApp's handheld scanner.
  • No rekeying of incoming LPO information.
  • Track LPOs as Received or Issued.
  • Record the service RO and quantity issued.
  • Review Received and Issued LPOs online or export them to Excel.
  • View LPOs currently in transit using the Shipped but Not Received report.
  • Link directly to Parts Workbench Plus.
  • No DMS interface required.

LPO Labels

Print labels showing VINL6, part information and a barcode that can be scanned when issuing the LPO.

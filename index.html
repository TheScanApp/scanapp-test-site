GM LPO Management

Track dealer-installed option parts from GM shipment through Parts receipt and optional issuance to a service RO. No DMS interface is required.

  • GM shipment data provides incoming LPO information automatically.
  • Receive LPOs with ScanApp's handheld scanner.
  • Track Received, Issued and Remaining quantities.
  • Optionally record the service RO when an LPO is issued.
  • Review Received and Issued LPOs together or separately.
  • Print labels with VINL6, part information and a barcode.
  • Scan the label barcode as an alternative way to issue the LPO.
  • See LPOs shipped by GM but not yet received.
  • Link directly to Parts Workbench Plus.
  • Export LPO information to Excel and retain historical records.

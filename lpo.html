Stop LPO chaos. Take back control.

ScanApp tracks GM LPO parts from shipment by GM through receipt by the Parts Department. ScanApp also tracks LPO issuance to a service RO (optional).

Isolate. Track. Control. Every LPO. Every time.

The ScanApp LPO Solution

A simple process to keep every LPO accounted for from shipment through receipt and optional issuance.

IDENTIFY

GM shipment data automatically provides all incoming LPO information.
RECEIVE

Parts are received using ScanApp’s handheld scanner, which records the shipment as an LPO on an easy-to-use and accessible web interface.
LABEL
(Optional Printer)

Print VINL6, part information and a barcode to attach to the received part for easier physical searching and locating.
TRACK

LPOs remain in Received status until issued, with quantities and status visible in ScanApp.
ISSUE

Record the service RO when issued to Service, or scan the barcode on the ScanApp label as an alternative method of issuing the LPO.

Everything needed to control LPOs

  • GM shipment data automatically provides all incoming LPO information.
  • All information except the service RO and quantity issued is recorded automatically. No need to rekey a thing.
  • Received and Issued LPOs can be reviewed separately or together online or through an exported Excel spreadsheet.
  • All LPO data is held and is never purged.
  • ScanApp links directly to Parts Workbench Plus for easy review of Part Numbers, Shipment Numbers or VINL6s.
  • A Shipped but Not Received report shows all LPOs currently in transit.
  • Users have a Notes field attached to each LPO for comments or reference information.
  • All LPO records are maintained digitally in ScanApp, outside of the dealership's operational DMS inventory.
  • No DMS interface required.

Real benefits across the dealership

Parts Department

Keep operational inventory clean, reduce time spent locating and managing LPOs, and create accountability.

Sales Department

Faster access to accessories for sold vehicles, fewer delivery delays and better visibility by VIN.

Service Department

Better visibility into issued LPOs, clearer communication and easier retrieval of the correct part.

From receipt to issue, every LPO is tracked, accountable and easy to find.

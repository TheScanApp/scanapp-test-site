Stop LPO chaos. Take back control.

ScanApp tracks GM LPO parts from shipment through Parts receipt and optional issuance to a service RO — without requiring a DMS interface.

The ScanApp LPO solution

GM shipment data automatically provides incoming LPO information. Parts are received using ScanApp's handheld scanner and maintained digitally outside the dealership's operational DMS inventory.

1. Identify
Incoming LPO information from GM shipment data.
2. Receive
Scan the part with ScanApp.
3. Label
Print VINL6, part information and barcode.
4. Track
Maintain Received, Issued and Remaining quantities.
5. Issue
Optionally record service RO or scan the label barcode.

Visibility that remains available

Review Received and Issued LPOs separately or together online or through an exported Excel spreadsheet. All LPO data is held and is never purged. A Shipped but Not Received report shows LPOs currently in transit. Users also have a Notes field attached to each LPO.

ScanApp links directly to Parts Workbench Plus for easy review of Part Numbers, Shipment Numbers or VINL6s.
